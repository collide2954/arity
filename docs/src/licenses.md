# Licenses

## The code

The code of arity is under the Apache License 2.0. See the file `LICENSE`.

## The data of arity

The records that arity makes are under CC0 1.0: the submissions, the checks,
and the records of the works, the files, the texts, and the notices. You can
use them for any purpose, without conditions. The full texts, the PDFs, and
the quotes are not records of arity, and they keep the licenses of their
sources.

## The sources

Each source keeps its own license. arity records the license of each work from
its JATS XML or from the PMC open-access store.

- The website shows the full text and the PDF of a work only when its license
  lets others share it, for example a Creative Commons license or CC0. For a
  work with a license that arity cannot read, the website and the copy of the
  database leave out the full text and the PDF. The metadata and the quotes of
  the reports stay.
- A quote in a submission is a short part of its source. It stays under the
  license of its source, and the website shows the citation and the license of
  the work next to it.
